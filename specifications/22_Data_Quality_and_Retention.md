# 22_Data_Quality_and_Retention.md — Data Quality & Retention Policy Specification

## 1. Purpose
This document specifies data validation policies, deduplication rules, HIPAA data retention schedules, and automated purging policies across HEMP.

---

## 2. Retention Rules
- Audit Logs & Financial Remittances: Retained for 7 Years (HIPAA & CMS Compliance).
- Temporary Sessions & Cache Records: Purged automatically after 24–48 hours.
