# Claims Module — Functional Requirements

## 1. Scope
The Claims Module manages electronic 837 claim intake, pre-adjudication edits, fee schedule pricing, adjudication workflows, and 835 remittance generation.

## 2. Requirements
- **FR-CLM-01**: Ingestion of 837P (Professional) and 837I (Institutional) claims.
- **FR-CLM-02**: Automated CCI (Correct Coding Initiative) and duplicate check edits.
- **FR-CLM-03**: Claim adjudication pricing engine for clean claim auto-adjudication.
