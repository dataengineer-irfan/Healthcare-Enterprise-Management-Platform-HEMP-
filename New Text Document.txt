One important note

As we get into implementation, I'll distinguish between:

Common enterprise design patterns (which we can adopt freely),
Public healthcare standards (such as FHIR, X12, and public Medicaid guidance), and
Organization-specific or proprietary workflows, which we'll design ourselves rather than attempting to reproduce from any existing healthcare portal.

That approach gives you an original, maintainable platform with realistic healthcare functionality while respecting the boundaries between public standards and proprietary implementations.

AI Governance Principles

Every AI action should follow these principles:

Authenticate the user.
Authorize the requested action.
Resolve business entities through the semantic catalog.
Execute only approved APIs or governed queries.
Log the action.
Return an explainable result.

This makes AI behavior auditable and consistent.
